# Welcome surface

Welcome is a distinct Grok surface with compact/full/hero variants, version
badge, quit chords, model caption, and action hints. Runie keeps it separate
from the settled transcript and removes it on the first submitted event.

Reference: `src/views/welcome/` in Grok and `widgets/welcome.rs` in Runie.
Acceptance: welcome snapshots and `visual-welcome-badges.yaml`.
